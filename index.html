NORMIE HOPPER

Every Normie is a level — #0 is Level 1, and clearing a face loads the next. Only the white pixels hold weight; ropes bridge the long gaps. From Level 10 enemies patrol the faces — a new kind every level, and they push. Faster runs score more, falls cost you.

Touch: left stick to move, orange button to jump.
Keys: WASD / arrows + space.
